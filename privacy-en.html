Privacy Policy — LandMeasure

Effective 2 November 2026 · version 1.0 · app com.landmeasure.app
In short: the app has no sign-up and no account. Measurements, coordinates, photos and voice notes are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and are never uploaded. The only outbound traffic is a weather request (coordinates rounded to about 1 km) and map tiles.

1. Who processes the data

LandMeasure (localized as «ПолеМер» in Russian, «ПолеМір» in Ukrainian, «ЖерӨлшер» in Kazakh) is developed and published by an individual developer. Contact: codeveil3@gmail.com.

2. Data created and stored on the device

  • Measurement point coordinates (latitude, longitude), computed area and perimeter.
  • Field name, comment, measurement history, measurement method, date.
  • Photos you add to a measurement.
  • Voice notes recorded when you tap “Record”.
  • App settings: language, units, irrigation parameters.

All of it is kept in the app's private storage on the device: the database is encrypted (SQLCipher), photos and audio are stored encrypted. The developer has no access to this data and receives no copies.

3. What leaves the device

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) Coordinates rounded to two decimals — about 1 km. No device or user identifiers. 7-day weather forecast and water-balance calculation for the field.
OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) The requested map tile number and the device IP address (as with any website). Map display while walking the boundary.
Esri ArcGIS (server.arcgisonline.com) The requested satellite tile number and the device IP address. Satellite layer when placing points.

These requests are only made for the relevant feature. Offline, the app still works: measurements, history, area calculations and export are available; the map and forecast are not.

4. What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no social login.
  • No analytics, usage statistics or tracking SDKs (Firebase, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data beyond the technical requests listed above.
  • No background operation and no location tracking without your action.

5. Permissions and why

  • Location — to record measurement points. Requested when you open a GPS measurement. You can decline: “By points” and “Manual” work without GPS.
  • Camera — to take a photo when you tap “Camera”.
  • Microphone — only after you tap “Record”. If declined, everything else keeps working.
  • Internet — map and weather forecast.

Gallery access uses the system photo picker: the app only sees the photos you select.

6. Backups

The app can export a backup file. It is created by your action, saved where you choose, and encrypted. The developer has no access to it.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. You can delete a measurement or an attached file in the app; uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Since the developer never receives your data, there is nothing to delete server-side — a deletion request amounts to deleting data on the device.

8. Children

The app is intended for adult users — farmers, agronomists, landowners — and is not directed to children. Children's data is not knowingly collected.

9. Your rights

You may know what is processed (section 2), delete it (section 7), and ask any question at codeveil3@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

10. Changes

If what the app processes changes, this policy is updated and the date above changes. Material changes will be shown in the app or in the Google Play release notes.

11. Measurement accuracy

Area is measured from GPS data. Accuracy depends on signal conditions (typically 3–10 m in open terrain) and does not replace a land survey or cadastral documents. The app warns about poor signal and offers manual input.
